Rotary Outreach Tracker
Quick Start Guide

Use this app to keep one shared view of local company outreach so Rotary members can see who is contacting whom, what each business has been asked to support, and what needs to happen next.

Dashboard See total companies, commitments, sponsorship progress, and opportunity counts.
Companies Search, filter, open records, import CSVs, and export lists.
Company Records View contact info, engagements, notes, and ownership in one place.
Resources Open sponsorship, vendor, silent auction, and marketing materials.

How To Use The App

Follow this simple workflow anytime you need to check a company, add outreach, or update progress.

1

Start on the Dashboard

Review the overall picture first. The dashboard shows how many companies are in the system, how many commitments have been made, and which opportunity types are active.

2

Search Before Contacting

Go to Companies and search the business name first. This helps avoid duplicate outreach and shows whether another Rotary member is already working that relationship.

Open the Company Record

Best Practices For Members

  • Always search the company name first before making contact.
  • Use the company record as the single source of truth for notes and follow-up.
  • Update the status as soon as a conversation changes.
  • Add a new engagement if the company is being asked about another opportunity.
  • Use clear notes so another member can step in without guessing.
If a company already exists but the name looks slightly different, pause and check the existing record before creating a new one.

Helpful Tools In The App

  • Add new company: Create a record for a business not yet in the list.
  • Import companies from CSV: Bring in existing company lists.
  • Download company list & assignments: Export current records.
  • Opportunity totals: Click a category on the dashboard to jump to filtered companies.
  • Marketing materials: Open sponsorship, vendor, and other support resources.
